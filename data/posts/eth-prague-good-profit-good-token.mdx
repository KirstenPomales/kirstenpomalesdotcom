I gave a talk at ETH Prague called *Good Profit, Good Token - Building the Next Era of Crypto Businesses*.

It's 25 minutes, and it's the spoken version of [the longer piece I wrote on this](/blog/good-profit-good-token).

The premise: over the past 10 years, crypto drifted from infra hype to governance tokens to memecoins. Along the way we eroded the original use case of tokens entirely: fundraising vehicles.

The future belongs to businesses with real revenue models, and tokens that distribute actual shareholder value.

In the talk I walk through concrete examples of teams already doing this. Not theory. Companies that have quietly been running this playbook for years, while everyone was looking somewhere else.

If you're building something with good profit and a good token, I want to hear about it.
